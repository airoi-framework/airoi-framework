## 0. About this document

How engagements are sized and what each side commits to. Tiers are **return-confidence levels**: how much certainty about Potential × Fit the client is buying — not feature lists. That the tiers deliver distinct, increasing confidence is a framework hypothesis (Spec 00 §1.3), tested at Recalibrate by comparing realized against predicted return per tier.

---

## 1. Tiers

| | **Pulse** | **Case** | **Launch** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Return-confidence** | directional signal — first estimate of return potential | full return case — defensible P × F with a roadmap | return case + realization preparation |
| **Stakeholders** | up to 6 | up to 12 | up to 18 |
| **Sessions** | Alignment + compressed field block | full lifecycle (Stage 0–3 + Retrospective) | full lifecycle + additional expert interviews |
| **Groundwork** | 2 research streams | all 4 streams | all 4 + vertical deep-dives |
| **Field scope** | 1–2 processes, light | 2–4 processes, full deep-dive | 4+ processes, extended |
| **Decision Report** | short form | full gradient | full + per-opportunity business cases, architecture recommendations, implementation backlog, 90-day plan |
| **Best for** | first conversation, smaller organizations, budget probe | organizations experimenting with AI that need structure | larger or regulated organizations preparing real change |

**What tiers never cut:** the Ambition Vector calibration (without Fit there is no return case at any confidence), the un-bias and psychological-safety principles, feedback records, and the Retrospective. The Recalibration Loop runs at every tier.

**Tier selection heuristics:**

- Existential-threat signals or regulated environment → not Pulse; the confidence floor is too low for the stakes.
- No sponsor identified → no tier; fix that first (Spec 02 §6 — intake refusal).
- Pulse engagements that validate demand upgrade to Case; the Pulse artifacts carry forward (the dossier extends rather than restarts).

## 2. Client commitments

The return is co-realized (Spec 00 §5.2); these commitments are the client's side of the equation. They are agreed at intake, per tier:

| Commitment | Means concretely |
|---|---|
| **Access** | the right people in the right sessions — decision-makers at Alignment and Decision, real Process Experts (not delegates-of-delegates) in the Field Sessions |
| **Information** | existing documents and hard data where available; honesty where data is absent |
| **Time** | roughly half a day per session per participant, plus short feedback forms |
| **Decisions** | at the Decision Session, leadership decides — proceed, park, or wait, but decides |
| **Measurement** | a follow-up interval for Recalibrate and access to the metrics agreed at sign-off |

Without these, the engagement still produces a document — but a research artifact, not a return case. The difference is stated up front, at intake, in exactly these terms.

## 3. Quality gates per tier

The full gate list lives in Spec 02 §5. Tier deltas:

| Gate | Pulse | Case | Launch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence Dossier before Stage 1 | reduced OK | mandatory full | mandatory full |
| Ambition Vector leadership sign-off | mandatory | mandatory | mandatory |
| Per-opportunity owner + pain anchor | shortlist only | mandatory all | mandatory all |
| Stakes + action level + oversight per shortlisted opportunity | mandatory | mandatory | mandatory |
| Acceptance Card per approved opportunity | short form | mandatory full | mandatory full |
| Roadmap validated against Absorption Rules | mandatory | mandatory | mandatory |
| AI Register handover | merged into report | mandatory | mandatory |
| Pre-read before final session | merged allowed | mandatory | mandatory |
| Recalibrate agreement at sign-off | mandatory | mandatory | mandatory |
| Business cases per priority opportunity | — | — | mandatory |

The invariant across all tiers: **no sign-off without an Ambition Vector, named owners, Acceptance Cards, and a Recalibrate agreement.** Those four are what make the output a return case rather than a report.

## 4. Adapting the framework

AIROI is CC-BY-SA: practitioners may adapt freely under the same license. Adaptations remain AIROI-conformant if they preserve:

1. The seven principles (Spec 01) — including P5 un-bias and P6 safety in any redesigned instruments
2. The chain closure property — engagements end with Acceptance Cards, an AI Register handover, and a Recalibrate agreement, not a report handoff
3. The data standard's honesty mechanics — Evidence Grades, the rationale contract, L0/N-A/skip distinctions, measured baselines
4. The red-flag pass before synthesis, and the three Decide gates (proportionality, acceptance, absorption)

Everything else — instruments, session formats, sector baselines, pool content, tier names — is expected to vary. The calibration assets a practitioner accumulates are their own.
